How TrustGuard AI Works

A rigorous 3-stage validation process turning raw high-volume financial transactions into actionable security decisions.

01
Ingestion & Vectorization

Incoming transactions are ingested with timestamps, transactional amounts, and 28 mathematical PCA features representing anonymized behavior vectors.

02
Ground Truth Classification

Transactions are verified against verified ground-truth labels. Normal transactions are cleared immediately, while high-risk anomalies are quarantined.

03
Analyst Review & Resolution

Bank analysts inspect anomalous vectors, assign case priorities, document forensic findings, and update case statuses to safeguard accounts.

Security Architecture

Engineered for Trust & Compliance

Built strictly with privacy-preserving techniques, robust authentication primitives, and audit-ready data safeguards.

Zero PII Leakage

Features V1-V28 are mathematical principal components. No raw account numbers, names, or addresses are stored in transaction records.

Cryptographic Password Hashing

All user and analyst credentials are encrypted with salted, adaptive cryptographic hashing to prevent dictionary attacks.

Session RBAC Guards

Server-side decorators rigorously isolate customer accounts and bank administration capabilities from unauthorized requests.

Read-Only Dataset

Ground truth transaction records in creditcard.csv remain immutable; case state and notes are managed in structured SQLite tables.
